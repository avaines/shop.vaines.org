About

Built by a maker, not a factory

I make every piece in this shop in small batches, with a focus on practical design and lasting quality. Vaines Shop grew from evenings in the workshop into a focused craft practice: thoughtful objects, clean finishes, and honest materials.

The shop runs through Etsy for ordering and delivery, but the making happens here: designed, tested, and refined one piece at a time.

Materials and finish standards

I choose materials for strength, feel, and how well they age. Depending on the piece, that can include solid timber, durable hardware, and hand-applied finishes selected for daily use rather than showroom only.

Before listing an item, I check the build quality, fit, and finish so what arrives matches the photographs and description.

My approach

The process is simple:

  1. Design for function first.
  2. Build in small runs to keep quality consistent.
  3. Review each piece before dispatch.

I value straightforward communication and clear product details, so you can buy with confidence.

Need something specific?

If you have a question about a listing, a finish, or a custom request, get in touch via the contact page. I will reply as quickly as possible.
